02 / The approach

Good science needs
more than a
convincing answer.

AI can help you write the analysis. The responsibility to question it stays human.

Vaibify makes that responsibility part of the workflow: inspect the figures, test the outputs, track what changed, and preserve the evidence behind what you share.

Reproducing a result is one kind of evidence. Deciding whether the methods and conclusions hold up still takes scientific judgment.
